Guide & rules

Your daily routine, your tools and the rules that keep your channel safe.

Our daily routine (about 1 hour each)

  1. Clipper 1 finds: pick the freshest GTA 6 stream from a campaign streamer and run it through Opus Clip
  2. Clipper 1 picks: choose the best reactions and moments, skip story cutscenes
  3. Clipper 2 polishes: write the hook, mute the radio, check captions, trim the start
  4. Clipper 2 posts: Shorts, TikTok and Reels, with the campaign hashtags
  5. Both log: submit the links and note views after 48 hours

Swap roles every Monday so neither of you gets bored. On a low-energy day, one clip each still counts.

GTA 6 rules that keep us safe

  1. No story cutscenes or the ending. Rockstar removes isolated cinematics and spoilers.
  2. No leaks. Nothing from before launch that isn't official.
  3. Mute the radio. Licensed songs trigger Content ID claims.
  4. No NPC-massacre compilations. YouTube can demonetize shock content.
  5. No GTA logo or Rockstar art as our channel branding, and no "official"-sounding name.
  6. Always add our own hook. Never post AI clips untouched.
  7. Never buy views, and remember Take-Two can take any video down.
  8. Split money in writing and talk to a tax advisor once money comes in.
